# Client style sheet — Harbour Tales Press

- Audience: adult general readers; warm, unhurried literary tone.
- Keep proper nouns (people, places, boats) untranslated.
- Keep narration in a neutral third person; dialogue may use informal forms where natural.
- Numbers: spell out numbers under one hundred in narration; keep digits for dates and times.
- Dialogue: use em dashes (—) for dialogue in Spanish, not quotation marks.
- Do not add footnotes or translator's notes.
- Preserve paragraph breaks exactly.
